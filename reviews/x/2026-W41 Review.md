### V1 — H1 forwardable number (Tue 13 Oct candidate)

Cooling-station spacing on an LH2 line is set by heat leak, not by a map pin.

Hold the line at 20 K. A 1 W/m heat leak on a 100 mm class cryostat is already ~86 MJ/day per km before transfer and valve loads. Station spacing is that load divided by the recondenser or vent budget, then cut again for elevation and idle.

If the model cannot show that division, it is drawing a picture.

### V2 — H2 correction (Wed 14 Oct candidate)

Superconducting cable in LH2 is not free cooling.

MgB2 wants ~20–25 K. LH2 can sit there. The cable still dumps AC loss, joint heat, and a quench into the same fluid that is the product. A quench that boils the coolant is a loss-of-coolant event, not a thermal freebie.

The constraint is heat into the LH2 stream, not the boiling point on a slide.

First reply: point at the quench / heat-load note only. Audience: cable OEM.
