# Phased build and research roadmap

Date: 2026-10-29. These are ordered work gates, not promises that a national audit can be completed in a fixed number of days.

## Gate 0 — premise and research boundary

Recheck the reward programme's written rules against the public statement; log differences and review date. Define the distinction between waste, error, overpayment, civil false claim, criminal fraud and recoverable loss. Verify that public-source independent analysis is materially new before treating it as a possible reward lead. Published examples train methods and establish typologies; they are not newly discovered tips.

Exit: reviewed reward-route matrix, legal-status vocabulary and public/restricted classification. No forecast of guaranteed payment or claimant eligibility.

## Gate 1 — portable evidence foundation

Create source, sector, method and published-case nodes; provenance edges; one note per node; local search and validation. Keep data/methods independent of any one model vendor. Produce a build report with source/node/edge/note counts, rejected records and known gaps. Tests must exercise real failure boundaries, not only happy-path formatting.

Exit: a fresh checkout can validate and rebuild the public research bank using documented commands. Engine foundation and seeds can be called delivered only after that succeeds. Agents remain specifications until a runner invokes them.

## Gate 2 — three complete bounded cohorts

| Cohort | Proposed boundary | Core joins | Initial tests | Stop conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Procurement product family | One awarding agency, one product/NSN family, one fiscal year, all identifiable awards | Award/action ↔ recipient UEI/CAGE ↔ product/unit ↔ public corporate identity | Competition and price comparability, repeat amendments, relevant debarment timing | Missing quantities/specifications prevent price comparison; public awards incomplete |
| DME or home-health | One public provider file, one state/region, one year and defined HCPCS/service group | NPI/CCN ↔ provider service aggregates ↔ public ownership/enrolment ↔ exclusions at service date | Peer-normalised utilisation, impossible chronology, ownership concentration candidates | Suppression, case-mix or inaccessible claims prevent inference; no patient-level authorization |
| Grants/single audit | One Assistance Listing or agency programme and fiscal period, all public awardees with matching audits | Award/FAIN ↔ UEI ↔ FAC audit ↔ public subrecipient/related-party disclosures | Repeated audit findings, overlap in costs only when documentation permits, incomplete remediation | Award-level totals cannot establish duplicate invoices; audit periods do not align |

These are research cohorts, not predetermined targets. Select exact boundaries from available source versions and document the choice before viewing rankings. Use all entities in the cohort, including low-risk controls. Avoid selection by notoriety or a desired allegation.

Exit per cohort: acquisition manifest; complete count reconciliation; identity QA sample; economic/clinical/grant-context review; reproducible test outputs; independently reviewed nulls and alerts; and a written coverage statement.

## Gate 3 — resolve the strongest justified paths

For each surviving alert, follow buyer/programme → award or reimbursement measure → recipient/provider → documented subcontractor or supplier → corporate ownership assertion valid at the relevant date → product/facility/business geography. Identify exactly where public evidence stops. Add only role-relevant public individuals if directly evidenced. Distinguish a corporate relationship from participation in misconduct.

Use a lead score to allocate research time, never as a probability of fraud. Suggested dimensions are evidence completeness, public-data detectability, financial materiality in a comparable measure, recency, testability, alternative-explanation burden and source novelty. Reward fit is a separate, uncertain routing field. Give explicit reasoning instead of a false-precision national risk leaderboard.

Exit: restricted packets for any novel named leads, falsification log and independent evidence review. The outcome can be `disproved`, `data_error`, `legitimate_explanation`, `unresolved` or `needs_official_records`.

## Gate 4 — scale by proven adapter

Add sectors in batches only after the first cohorts pass. Reuse identity/provenance/money controls but do not transfer numerical baselines across programmes. General procurement methods can apply to defence and medical supplies; clinical necessity and grant allowability require different specialists. Integrate state programmes per jurisdiction, not as one uniform national table.

Prioritise additional scopes by demonstrated exposure, availability of lawfully accessible data and ability to test a concrete hypothesis. Tax and UI losses may be large while decisive underlying records are inaccessible. Record that limitation rather than spending effort fabricating indirect proxies.

Exit: adapter conformance tests, source schema monitoring, shared restricted task service if necessary, cost/throughput measurement and correction/retention procedures.

## Gate 5 — operational decision support

Only after authorization, provision a runner with read-only source tools, scoped credentials, event logging, kill switch and explicit budgets. Add human-reviewed reporting workflows. A legal reviewer chooses an applicable reporting route and checks confidentiality/public-disclosure issues before external action. No automatic accusation, mass outreach, tip submission, reward application or publication is part of the default runner.

The immediate milestone is one complete reproducible cohort that another analyst can verify. The national bank grows through successful, bounded work packages; accumulating thousands of unreviewed notes is not evidence of auditing coverage.
