# Public source map for price investigations

Checked 30 October 2026. These are acquisition routes and limitations, not a declaration that every underlying record has been obtained. [Source observations](plans/source-observations.json) preserve retrieval depth; [the query ledger](plans/research-log.json) includes failed opens. No original webpage bytes were archived by this architecture lane.

| Source | What can be researched | Critical boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [USAspending API](https://api.usaspending.gov/) and [analyst guide](https://www.usaspending.gov/federal-spending-guide) | Awards and transactions, agency/recipient identifiers, cumulative obligations and reported subawards | Award summaries differ from transaction rows; neither is a complete line-item sales, invoice or bank ledger. Guide text was available in the official search index; direct open returned only a page shell. |
| [SAM Opportunities API documentation](https://open.gsa.gov/api/get-opportunities-public-api/) | Notices, opportunity metadata and public attachment pointers | The documented public API needs an API key, requires pagination and exposes the latest active notice version. Account/API access is not activated by this plan. Public notice pages and actually accessible documents remain separate routes. |
| [DLA PUB LOG](https://www.dla.mil/Information-Operations/Services/Applications/PUB-LOG/ProceduresManuals/) and prior inspected DIBBS lines | Exact procurement instruments and unrestricted item data where available | This lane's webpage open returned 403. The previous bolt investigation verified a public archive route but did not extract the exact NIIN. Generic standards and secondary dimensions do not close that gap. |
| [VA Contract Catalog Search Tool](https://www.vendorportal.ecms.va.gov/nac/) | Public product and contract pricing, ordering, delivery and warranty information | VA states public viewing/download is available and flags the catalog as reference information. Resolve discrepancies against the authorized contractor price list. A catalog price is not proof of a particular order or cash payment. |
| [GSA service-pricing guidance](https://www.gsa.gov/buy-through-us/products-and-services/professional-services/buy-services/oasis-plus/buyers-guide/buyers-guide-printable) | Public labor ceiling-rate context and method guidance | Public ceilings are not actual paid rates. The May 2026 guide says CALC+ Prices Paid is restricted to eligible federal users/contractors with .gov/.mil email. Do not assume access, substitute credentials or label ceiling comparisons paid-price analysis. |
| [FAR 13.106-3](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/13.106-3) | Price-reasonableness and quotation-record requirements relevant to simplified acquisitions | A catalog alone does not establish price reasonableness. Check the actual action's governing clauses, date and deviations; guidance does not itself prove noncompliance. |
| [SEC data APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) | Filer history, dated filings and financial-statement facts using CIK identifiers | Public data APIs need no key. Consolidated revenue and securities transactions do not reveal every vendor sale, ownership relationship or procurement payment. Read actual filings for the claimed relationship. |
| [SEC fair-access guidance](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data) | Targeted filing acquisition and indexes | Respect declared user-agent and request-rate policy; download only needed records. A corrected or removed filing may require a new observation rather than silently rewriting history. |
| [FinCEN current FAQ notice](https://www.fincen.gov/boi/beneficial-ownership-information-frequently-asked-questions) and [access framework](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fact-sheet-beneficial-ownership-information-access-and-safeguards-final-rule) | Determine source availability and avoid a fictitious public-owner lookup | BOI is not a public research database. The current FAQ reports a 2026 rule changing reporting scope, so do not assume a complete domestic-company register. State officers/registered agents and SEC disclosures remain distinct evidence types. |
| [DOJ collusion guidance](https://www.justice.gov/atr/red-flags-collusion) | Design tests of actual proposal similarities, winner patterns and subcontract relationships | Signals require actual relevant records and ordinary alternatives. A reported offer count or shared manufacturer is insufficient; no automated allegation or external report follows. |
| [FOIA overview](https://www.foia.gov/faq.html) | Locate already released records and describe exact missing agency records | Release is not guaranteed; exemptions and record availability matter, and agencies need not create analyses. Drafting a precise record description does not mean a request was sent. |

## Prioritized evidence routes

For each candidate: resolve the official award and parent/order/modification chain, obtain the actual priced line, and map the exact item or service. Then obtain an independent equivalent benchmark with date, quantity and terms. Follow corporate and professional-role links only when they bear on that transaction. Require separate released evidence for a benefit nexus. When a chain stops, save the exact missing record rather than connecting unrelated public names.

Public business filings may support a dated officer or owner assertion where actually disclosed. They do not guarantee ultimate ownership, acquisition costs, customer lists, bank flows or a buyer's private benefit. A supplier's sale of shares, fund holding or consolidated revenue is a separate relationship from a procurement sale. The earlier [ownership memo](../OWNERSHIP-AND-COMMERCE.md) provides these distinctions; the current source checks above qualify access today.

The current pilot covers DLA 5306, VA 6515 and HHS 6515 samples. Construction, services, fuel and logistics need their own documented unit and term controls before expansion. No source in this table provides everything across government, and unknown fields stay unknown.
