# US fraud rewards: verified baseline

Research checked: **29 October 2026**. This is a source-led research baseline, not a finding that a particular person qualifies for an award. URLs below were retrieved through web search/open; the source register distinguishes current guidance from proposals and archived statutory editions. No report was submitted and no target was contacted.

## The 1% claim

Trump announced the initiative on **20 October 2026**. Reuters reported an uncapped 1% promise, while the written DOJ announcement described $10,000–$30,000 discretionary awards. [R01, R02, R03] The public promise must therefore remain a **reported statement with unresolved implementation discrepancy**, not a universal contractual entitlement and not a revenue forecast.

The event transcript attributes to Trump: “if we recover the money” and “You get 1 percent.” [R24] That language concerns recoveries; it does not establish a general right to 1% of estimated future budget savings.

The DOJ announcement establishes WHIP, backed by a $25 million Treasury commitment, with awards following charges. It says participation can coexist with other reward programs. [R02] Detailed terms identify a three-year pilot under 28 U.S.C. §530C(b)(1)(L); payment can be declined in DOJ’s discretion. [R04]

**Internal scope inconsistency:** the detailed terms’ introductory agency list omits the Department of War, but the same document’s eligibility section 6 (Subject Area) includes it, as do the announcement, landing page and FAQ. Preserve the section references and seek clarification before relying on a defense-related reward. [R02–R05, R22]

## Routes to evaluate

| Route | Verified mechanism | Research implication |
|---|---|---|
| WHIP | $10,000–$30,000 at or around unsealed criminal charges, within covered program areas; discretionary. [R04, R05] | Prioritize actionable, specific evidence; charge-stage awards are distinct from recovery percentages. |
| False Claims Act / qui tam | Normally 15–25% when government proceeds; 25–30% when it does not. Statutory exceptions and reductions apply. [R06] | A hotline tip alone is not the same as bringing a qui tam case. |
| IRS | Generally 15–30% of attributable collected proceeds; mandatory-track thresholds are over $2m disputed proceeds and, for an individual taxpayer, over $200k gross income in a relevant year. Below those conditions, discretionary review may apply. [R08] | Public tax returns of exempt organizations are useful; private tax returns are not publicly accessible investigative data. |
| SEC | 10–30% of collected sanctions following a qualifying action ordering over $1m sanctions. [R10] | Applies to securities violations, not every government overpayment. |
| CFTC | 10–30% of collected sanctions; covered action exceeds $1m. [R12] | Commodity/derivatives jurisdiction must be established. |
| DOJ Corporate Whistleblower Awards pilot | Original truthful information leading to over $1m net forfeiture in covered corporate-crime areas. Guidance revised 30 July 2026; discretionary. [R14] | Do not reuse old 2024 eligibility assumptions without checking the revised guidance. |
| DOJ Antitrust / USPS | Voluntary original information producing at least $1m criminal fine or other recovery; presumptive 15–30% reward, discretionary. Postal nexus and MOU conditions apply. [R16, R17] | Bid-rigging research may fit, but parallel prices or common ownership alone do not prove an agreement. |
| FinCEN | Statutory AML/sanctions reward program exists; website says awards will be processed/paid after implementation regulation is finalized. April 2026 rule is a proposal. [R18] | Store implementation as pending/unconfirmed; do not present proposed payout procedure as final. |

## Originality and publication

WHIP requires voluntary, truthful, complete written original information, cooperation and a qualifying individual. Its terms exclude certain officials/relatives, meaningful participants and unlawful/privileged information, subject to specified exceptions. Independent analysis can use public materials if it reveals information not generally known, materially adding to government knowledge. [R04] Its FAQ says simply repeating court/news allegations does not establish originality; identical later tips are not original. [R05]

FCA complaints start under seal for at least 60 days, with material evidence served on the government; related pending actions and public disclosures can bar a case, subject to statutory provisions including original-source rules. [R06] A federal court’s published decision explains that a self-represented relator cannot prosecute the government’s qui tam claim. [R07] **Obtain US whistleblower counsel before publicizing a novel case theory or trying to file.** This is the recommended workflow, not a claim that every public dataset or note is legally confidential.

SEC independent analysis requires insights beyond pointing to publicly available documents. Use the required TCR channel; an anonymous award submission requires attorney representation. Award applications normally have a 90-day covered-action deadline. [R11] CFTC also has TCR and WB-APP stages and a 90-day application window. [R12]

## Reporter location

US citizenship/residence is not required for IRS awards according to an October 2026 IRS statement. [R09] The SEC has paid joint whistleblowers located abroad. [R13] These facts do **not** establish this user’s eligibility for WHIP, FCA, SEC or other programs; jurisdiction, employment, information acquisition, tax treatment and individual exclusions need separate review. WHIP expressly excludes an elected or appointed foreign government official who acquired information in that status. [R04]

## Interpret the money correctly

GAO distinguishes fraud from improper payments: incorrect payments can reflect error, underpayment or missing documentation; fraud involves willful misrepresentation. Improper-payment sampling is not a measurement of fraud. [R19] GAO’s $233bn–$521bn annual estimate was published in April 2024 using FY2018–FY2022 data; it is not a newly found recoverable pot or a 2026 program-level loss figure. [R20]

Use separate numeric fields for **award obligation, payment, questioned cost, improper-payment estimate, alleged loss, adjudicated loss, settlement amount, collected recovery, prevented loss and verified whistleblower award**. Never multiply the total contract ceiling, GAO estimate or suspected saving by 1% and call that earned income. Those are engineering controls recommended for this project.

## Required system gates

1. **Source gate:** record source URL, publication and access dates, source status, exact page/paragraph and minimal necessary excerpt; preserve a permitted source snapshot/checksum if retrievable.
2. **Identity gate:** separate organization, subsidiary, owner, officer and government buyer; ownership must be time-bounded and sourced. A shared address is only a candidate link.
3. **Novelty gate:** record the analyst’s new contribution, known proceedings and existing disclosures; do not certify that agencies do not know a fact merely because no public case is found.
4. **Adversarial review:** require an alternative explanation and the next evidence that could falsify the lead.
5. **Human/counsel gate:** review jurisdiction, originality, possible duplication, privilege, seal, deadlines and admissibility before any external submission or publication of a novel allegation.
6. **Reward gate:** mark reward eligibility `not_assessed` until competent review; never promise a payment.

The gates are project design recommendations. No scheduled monitoring, ongoing background agents, inter-session synchronization or external filings were activated by this research.
