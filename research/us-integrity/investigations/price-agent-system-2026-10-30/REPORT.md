# Targeted price-investigation system and first federal pilot

Updated 30 October 2026. The user's concealed 12% markup example is now the basis for an executable investigation workflow: compare the same purchase on equivalent terms, test recurrence, then separately establish any purchasing influence or benefit. The system records what would disprove a lead as well as what could strengthen it.

Seven specialist workers completed implementation, acquisition, party tracing, specification follow-up, architecture and independent challenge. The resulting [agent system](../../price-agents/README.md) has **14 role contracts and 20 current tasks**, with eight gated expansion waves. Actual pilot work acquired 30 official awards, three detailed traces and an original two-line DLA order. This is a bounded investigation and working local coordination software, not nationwide coverage or a continuously running model fleet.

## Strongest new price evidence

The [DLA/VA/HHS pilot](../federal-price-pilot-2026-10-30/REPORT.md) reached DLA order **SPE4A626PW775**, signed 9 July 2026. Its production line is **2,243 EA × $272.99 = $612,316.57** for special front-engine-mount bolt **NSN 5306004457358 / part 39-8820-501**. A separate first-article test costs **$3,500**, bringing the order total to **$615,816.57**. The apparent subtotal difference is explained by that separate deliverable. Neither the combined quantity on the cover nor the two test articles should be silently added to production quantity.

The original contract identifies Don Industrial Group LLC, CAGE745V4, as dealer and SPS Technologies LLC, CAGE27624, as intended approved manufacturer. [Official identifier crosswalks and dated roles](../federal-price-pilot-2026-10-30/parties/REPORT.md) preserve this contractual chain separately from actual delivery, invoice and payment. The narrow printed professional role on the instrument does not establish who selected the price or received a benefit. Reported parent relationships are not a complete beneficial-ownership chain.

The source has drawing, quality, packaging, first-article and future-delivery requirements. Exact-part market searches found pointers to supplier listings but **zero acquired priced offers and zero qualified independent comparators**. No excessive markup, fair-value conclusion, payment, kickback or loss can be calculated from the acquired evidence. The prior **$0.91** bolt is a different NSN and remains a separate open comparison.

VA order36C79126F0030 and NIH order75N98026F00268 are traced to their reported suppliers and governing contract paths. Their actual model/quantity/unit-price lines remain unacquired. Two official catalog entries under the NIH supplier's schedule were explicitly rejected because they described different equipment. All 30 pilot rows remain in the declared incomplete sample; there was no search for a dramatic price by quietly changing the cohort.
