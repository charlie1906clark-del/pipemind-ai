## What the congressional evidence establishes

The bounded searches did **not** identify an enacted universal entitlement to 1% of government savings, or an identifiable general 1% bill. That is not proof that no such proposal exists. None of the legislative records retrieved converts discovery of an anomaly into an automatic award. An administration announcement, a member’s press release, a bill introduction and a law are separate graph nodes.

The DOJ authority cited for public rewards, **28 USC530C(b)(1)(L)**, has no percentage formula. Its ordinary reward ceiling is **$3 million**, with exceptions for terrorism or a statute authorizing more. Rewards of $250,000 or more need personal approval from the Attorney General or President; congressional committee leaders must receive notice within 30 days. The statute excludes judicial review of nonpayment and the amount. Presidential approval is therefore not, by itself, authority for an unlimited percentage reward. [C01]

The full statutory text opened here is the GPO 2024 edition, corroborated by current House search-indexed text. Direct House current-code requests failed. The captured edition and retrieval limitations must stay attached to the rule; this report does not silently label an archived edition as an October 2026 consolidation.

## Existing reward routes and the novelty gate

The **False Claims Act** is a civil false-claims mechanism that can include defense, health and other government-program payments. DOJ describes private qui tam suits as one route to recovery; merely reporting waste is not equivalent to bringing a successful action. [C04]

Under §3730, the usual shares are 15–25% when the government proceeds and 25–30% when it does not. These are percentages of action/settlement proceeds. Special reductions and exclusions apply. A complaint is initially sealed for at least 60 days and evidence is served on the government. A related pending action can block a later private action. Publicly disclosed allegations can trigger dismissal unless the government opposes or the claimant qualifies as an original source; independent knowledge that materially adds and is voluntarily provided before filing is one statutory route. Some primarily disclosed cases have a 10% ceiling. A convicted participant receives no share. [C02]

FCA knowledge can include deliberate ignorance or reckless disregard; specific intent to defraud is not generally required. A higher price alone does not establish a knowingly false claim or material false statement. Internal Revenue Code claims are excluded from §3729. [C03]

| Existing program | General reward basis | Boundary relevant to this project |
|---|---|---|
| IRS | Generally 15–30% of attributable collected proceeds | Laws within IRS administration/enforcement; eligible information, not general procurement savings [C19] |
| SEC | 10–30% of collected sanctions, qualifying action over $1m sanctions | Federal securities violations and original information [C20] |
| CFTC | 10–30% of collected sanctions, qualifying action over $1m sanctions | Commodity Exchange Act and qualifying related actions; voluntary original information [C21] |

These programs do not create interchangeable reward rates. Eligibility, originality, causation and collection remain program-specific. No eligibility conclusion about the user is made here.

For each new lead, store `first_seen_at`, source publication date, source retrieval date, exact allegation/transaction fingerprint, known court/DOJ/OIG/GAO/council disclosures, matches to earlier claims, what the analysis independently adds, and unresolved gaps. A failed public search supports **not located in the searched sources**, not **unknown to government**. A new name-normalization hit or price outlier is a candidate for investigation; it does not itself establish legally original information. Any filing or award review is a separate decision from repository research.

## Current procurement authority changes the audit design

**Do not implement a single $2.5 million certified-data cutoff.** PL119-60 §1804(c) amended 10 USC3702: qualifying prime contracts entered into after **30 June 2026** use a **$10 million** threshold. The earlier statutory baseline is $2 million, while official regulatory/deviation excerpts use the inflation-adjusted $2.5 million figure. Prime awards, modifications and subcontracts have distinct text. [C11–C13]

The official 2026-O0048 deviation PDF was available to search indexing but direct retrieval returned 502/503. Consequently, the full revision, effective date, and all exceptions have not been independently audited here. A later agent must attach the applicable contract clause and agency deviation version before computing a compliance flag. Nontraditional-contractor, competition, commercial-product/service, statutory-price and waiver exceptions require specific checking; missing public certified-cost data is not proof of unlawful withholding.

FAR15.404-1 expressly requires a valid comparator and adjustments for materially different quantities, terms and market conditions. Even where certified data are not required, price reasonableness analysis may use other data. Record exact item/NSN and revision, unit and package size, quality/certification, quantity, delivery date, freight, warranty, contract type, competition and acquisition date before comparing prices. Preserve uncertainty where any is missing. [C14]

FAR15.407-1 treats inaccurate, incomplete or noncurrent certified data that increased price as a potential contractual defective-pricing issue. That differs from a demonstrated false claim. Keep separate outcomes: unusual price, inadequate comparison, unreasonable price finding, contractual recovery, suspected knowing misstatement, and adjudicated/settled fraud. Commercial margins, sole sourcing and a company-name punctuation difference do not alone resolve those categories. [C15, C03]

## Operational consequences for the graph

Represent `Person -> made_statement -> Statement -> proposes -> Proposal`, `Bill -> has_action -> LegislativeAction`, and `PublicLaw -> amends -> Statute`. Only enactment evidence creates `is_binding_authority`. Represent an anomaly as `supports_question`, not `proves_fraud`. A price-comparison node needs comparator-validity and rule-version edges. Reward entries need program authority, proceeds basis, jurisdiction, date and unresolved eligibility fields. The absence of a universal 1% authority must not prevent useful public-data research; it prevents the system from presenting speculative rewards as earned money.

Unresolved items are the Cline proposal’s official number/status, the pricing proposal’s official bill crosswalk, full current DFARS deviation/revision, direct current-code text, and any legislative actions after the captured status update dates. No new fraud allegation is established by this authority workstream.
