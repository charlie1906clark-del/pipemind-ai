The October 2026 SBA statement also reports about $122 billion in suspected-fraud referrals and about $50 billion associated with borrower suspensions. These are agency-reported administrative/suspected measures, not a newly verified loss total. Their overlap is not reconciled here, and they must not be added to the $200 billion estimate. The [23 September 2026 SBA/IRS announcement](https://legacy.sba.gov/article/2026/09/23/sba-referral-leads-irs-investigate-tax-liabilities-related-potentially-100-billion-covid-loan-fraud) says approximately $100 billion in loans had tax-information discrepancies; individual examinations remained ongoing. Loan value associated with a discrepancy does not itself quantify tax loss or prove every loan fraudulent.

The [March 2025 OIG update](https://legacy.sba.gov/document/report-25-10-covid-19-pandemic-eidl-ppp-loan-fraud-landscape-recommendations-update) reiterates the original estimate while reviewing corrective recommendations; it is not an independent additional $200 billion. A [GAO referral-control review](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-25-107267) documents deficient earlier EIDL referrals and reports a February 2026 agency agreement whose operational evidence GAO was still awaiting. Neither source supplies a named undiscovered borrower case for this investigation.

These aggregate figures motivate careful investigation. They do not supply missing transaction evidence or imply that any particular supplier is fraudulent. No national amount is allocated to this $824.46 order, extrapolated from it, or treated as a recoverable pot.

## What would strengthen or disprove the lead

1. Obtain the applicable primary NIIN characteristics and reference-part relationship, then test every material technical, unit/pack, date, stock, delivery, certification, inspection, freight and contract-term requirement. A mismatch rejects that comparator; it does not prove fair pricing.
2. Obtain an actual feasible dated comparable offer or historical line under equivalent terms. Keep full-carton purchase cost, extra services and any negative difference. Do not calculate a target 12% benchmark from the government price.
3. Reconstruct the actual competing bids and price-reasonableness decision. Reported offer counts and a printed officer role do not establish independent price selection. A lowest-priced bid is not itself proof of fair value or absence of collusion.
4. Keep buyer influence/benefit as a separate evidence branch. No buyer benefit, payment, supplier-cost markup, loss or undiscovered status is established in the selected chain. Do not infer a personal connection from names or ordinary business roles.
