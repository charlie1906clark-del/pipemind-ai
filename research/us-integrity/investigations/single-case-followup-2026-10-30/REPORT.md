## How the price question must be tested

The actual ordered value is **$824.46**. A posted price for 1,100 commercial pieces cannot be converted into an overcharge simply by comparing advertised per-piece figures. Full-carton cost, leftover inventory, exact item conformity, applicable packaging/marking, delivery, freight and contract obligations need explicit treatment. Nor should additional governmental costs be assumed to exist or be expensive without evidence. The market lane retains full-carton arithmetic and clearly hypothetical residual budgets; those are not supplier costs, profit, loss or recoverable amounts.

The parent's estimated annual demand of **2,221 EA** is an evaluation quantity, not an actual annual purchase. H06 also contains a separate order-quantity rule. Using an annual-demand tier indiscriminately for every child order would create a false comparison. The lower $0.91 current price relative to the earlier history also does not establish fair market value or independent buying.

The supplier's legitimate identity, a reconciled order, the number of reported offers and a contracting role do not establish independence. Conversely, the acquired evidence contains no documented concealed 12% arrangement, personal benefit, false statement or knowing improper selection. A generalized national fraud estimate supplies none of those missing facts.
