# PipeMind AI

**AI software that helps energy companies plan, design, optimise and safely operate hybrid liquid-hydrogen + superconducting energy pipelines.**

This repository contains the complete research foundation, detailed module specifications, and development plans for PipeMind AI.

## The 6 Modules

1. **Smart Route Planner** – Multi-criteria corridor routing
2. **Cooling & Cryogenics Optimiser** – Thermo-hydraulic design
3. **Safety & Risk Simulator** – LH₂ + quench risk analysis
4. **Cost & Business Case Engine** – Full financial modelling
5. **Digital Twin Viewer** – Design and operational twin
6. **Report & Compliance Generator** – Automated professional reports
