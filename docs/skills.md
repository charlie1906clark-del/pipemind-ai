# PipeMind AI – Complete Skill System

Fourteen specialist Grok skills have been created to support every aspect of building and scaling PipeMind AI.

## Expert / Build Skills (for designing and coding the modules)

| Skill | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| `pipemind-master` | Overall strategy, architecture, prioritisation, business growth |
| `pipemind-route` | Building Module 1 – Smart Route Planner |
| `pipemind-cooling` | Building Module 2 – Cooling & Cryogenics |
| `pipemind-safety` | Building Module 3 – Safety & Risk Simulator |
| `pipemind-cost` | Building Module 4 – Cost & Business Case |
| `pipemind-twin` | Building Module 5 – Digital Twin Viewer |
| `pipemind-reports` | Building Module 6 – Report & Compliance |

## Research Skills (for expanding knowledge)

| Skill | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| `pipemind-research-route` | Fresh research on corridor routing, GIS, multi-criteria methods |
| `pipemind-research-cooling` | Fresh research on thermo-hydraulics, heat leak, station spacing |
| `pipemind-research-safety` | Fresh research on LH₂ leaks, quench, HyRAM+, NFPA 2, PRESLHY |
| `pipemind-research-cost` | Fresh research on CAPEX/OPEX, unit rates, competitiveness thresholds |
| `pipemind-research-twin` | Fresh research on energy digital twins and visualisation |
| `pipemind-research-reports` | Fresh research on automated engineering documentation & compliance |
| `pipemind-research-domain` | Broad research on SCARLET, hybrid concepts, MgB₂/REBCO, market |

## How to use them

Simply ask questions in natural language. Examples:

- “Research the latest heat-leak values for LH₂ cryostats” → activates `pipemind-research-cooling`
- “Help me implement the cost surface for Module 1” → activates `pipemind-route`
- “What is the current status of the SCARLET demonstrator?” → activates `pipemind-research-domain`
- “What should I prioritise this month?” → activates `pipemind-master`

The skills keep all advice consistent with the detailed research and build plans stored in this repository.
