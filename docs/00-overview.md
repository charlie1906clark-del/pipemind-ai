# PipeMind AI – Overview

## What we are building

PipeMind AI is specialised software that helps large energy companies plan, design, optimise and safely operate hybrid liquid-hydrogen + superconducting energy pipelines (SCEPs).

These pipelines carry both:
- Near-zero-loss electricity (MgB₂ or REBCO superconductors cooled to ~20 K)
- Chemical energy in the form of liquid hydrogen

## Why this can become a large business

- Hybrid pipelines are a real emerging technology (SCARLET project + multiple academic designs)
- The engineering is complex and currently done slowly with general tools
- Digital twin and energy planning software markets are growing rapidly
- Almost no specialist software exists specifically for hybrid LH₂ + superconducting systems

## Core Principles

1. Physics first, AI second
2. Every calculation must be transparent and auditable
3. Modular design – each module usable independently or together
4. Built for enterprise (long sales cycles, high trust required)
5. Keep the repository and personal knowledge system extremely organised

## The 6 Modules

| # | Module | Main Job |
|---|--------|----------|
| 1 | Smart Route Planner | Find the best corridor along major roads |
| 2 | Cooling & Cryogenics | Calculate temperatures, pressure, station spacing |
| 3 | Safety & Risk | Model leaks, quenches and quantify risk |
| 4 | Cost & Business Case | Full CAPEX/OPEX and comparison to alternatives |
| 5 | Digital Twin Viewer | Design and operational twin |
| 6 | Report & Compliance | Generate professional, audit-ready documents |

## Recommended build order

1. Safety (Module 3) or Route (Module 1) – highest credibility
2. Cooling (Module 2)
3. Cost (Module 4)
4. Digital Twin and Reports
