# PipeMind AI – Overview

## What we are building

PipeMind AI is specialised software that helps large energy companies plan and de-risk hybrid energy pipelines. These pipelines carry both electricity (via superconductors cooled by liquid hydrogen) and the liquid hydrogen itself.

The software does not dig trenches or lay pipe. It does the hard thinking: route selection, thermal design, safety analysis, cost modelling, digital twins, and professional reporting.

## Why this can become a large company

- Hybrid pipelines are a real emerging technology (SCARLET project and multiple academic designs exist)
- The engineering is complex and currently done slowly by hand or with general tools
- Digital twin and energy planning software markets are growing rapidly
- There is almost no specialist software focused specifically on hybrid LH₂ + superconducting systems

## Core Principles

1. Physics first, AI second
2. Every calculation must be transparent and auditable
3. Modular design – each module can be used independently or together
4. Built for enterprise (long sales cycles, high trust required)
5. Keep the repository and knowledge system extremely organised

## The 6 Modules (Summary)

| # | Module | Main Job |
|---|--------|----------|
| 1 | Smart Route Planner | Find the best corridor along major roads |
| 2 | Cooling & Cryogenics | Calculate temperatures, pressure, station spacing |
| 3 | Safety & Risk | Model leaks, quenches, and quantify risk |
| 4 | Cost & Business Case | Full CAPEX/OPEX and comparison to alternatives |
| 5 | Digital Twin Viewer | Visual and operational twin of the system |
| 6 | Report & Compliance | Generate professional, audit-ready documents |

## Recommended build order

1. Safety & Risk (Module 3) or Route (Module 1) – highest credibility
2. Cooling (Module 2)
3. Cost (Module 4)
4. Digital Twin and Reports later
