# Module 6 – Report & Compliance Generator
## Super-Detailed Build Plan

### Goal of v1
One-click generation of a complete, professional, audit-ready report pack.

### Phase 1 – Templates
1. Structured templates for the five main report types
2. Defined sections, required tables and figures
3. Support for client logo and basic branding

### Phase 2 – Automatic Population
4. Pull maps, profiles, risk results, cost tables and assumption registers from other modules
5. Generate a plain-language executive narrative
6. Maintain full version history

### Phase 3 – Output Quality
7. High-quality PDF export
8. Optional Word and PowerPoint export
9. Every number hyper-linked or foot-noted back to its origin

### Definition of Done
- Complete report pack generated in under five minutes
- Zero transcription errors between calculations and final document
- Acceptable to a client quality or regulatory reviewer without major rework
