## 2. Cooling & Cryogenics Advances
- GenH2 + ITP Interpipe: Successful thermal qualification of Izoflex soft-vacuum pipe-in-pipe insulation under real liquid hydrogen conditions on the Cryostat CS900 platform (ASTM C1774). Validated for commercial LH2 pipeline applications.
- Hybrid cable designs (Politecnico di Torino / SCARLET lineage) continue to target outer cryostat heat load < 2 W/m.
- Multiple roadmap and design papers reinforce MgB2 + LH2 as the practical combination for hybrid energy pipelines.
- New Chinese work on triple-phase coaxial LH2-cooled HTS energy pipelines showing significantly lower losses than conventional high-voltage AC lines.

**Upgrade:** Soft-vacuum pipe-in-pipe systems are moving from concept to validated technology. Heat-leak target of ≤ 2 W/m remains the design standard for long hybrid cables.
