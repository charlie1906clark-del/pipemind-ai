# Deep Research Update – Module 2 Cooling – 2026-08-04

## Topic
Validated heat-leak values, mass-flow requirements and transient behaviour for MgB₂-LH₂ hybrid cables.

## Key Findings
- Design target heat load in recent Italian submarine designs and SCARLET-related work: ≤ 2 W/m for the outer cryostat.
- For a 30 km, 10 kA / 30 kV MgB₂ cable carrying up to 1 kg/s LH₂, minimum mass flow to stay below 29 K outlet temperature is 0.5–0.6 kg/s.
- LOCA (Politecnico di Torino 2026): mid-cable rupture can drive parasitic heat load up by 1–2 orders of magnitude once annulus pressure reaches the mbar range (molecular conduction dominates). Boiling at outlet possible for outflows ~500 g/s under 2 W/m baseline.
- New MLI calorimeter data at true LH₂ temperatures: 50-layer ≈ 9.92 × 10⁻⁵ W/(m·K); 60-layer ≈ 1.45 × 10⁻⁴ W/(m·K).
- Indirect (helium-filled protective pipe) cooling remains viable up to ~20 W/m on the copper former while keeping T_Cu < 35 K.

## Knowledge Upgrade for Expert Skill
- Default steady-state heat leak: 1.5–2.0 W/m.
- Minimum mass-flow rule-of-thumb for ~30 km / ~300 MW class: ≥ 0.5 kg/s to stay < 29 K.
- LOCA must be modelled with vacuum degradation; parasitic load can jump 10–100×.
- Current-lead cold-end heat can be held to ≤ 150 W (negligible vs cable load).
