# Deep Research Update – Module 4 Cost – 2026-08-04

## Topic
Precise competitiveness thresholds and cost structure for hybrid SCEPs.

## Key Findings
- TEMOA-Italy 2026: hybrid SCEP is cost-effective when specific investment cost falls below ≈ 610 €/km/MW.
- If conventional transmission costs double, the threshold rises to ≈ 1430 €/km/MW.
- Baseline cable cost used in scaling is ~1.0–1.1 M€/km before normalisation by total (electric + H₂-equivalent) power.
- In detailed HTS cable cost breakdowns, superconducting tape becomes the dominant CAPEX item at high power (up to 58 % at multi-GW).
- Cooling stations and civil works remain major non-tape cost drivers.

## Knowledge Upgrade for Expert Skill
- Primary go/no-go threshold: 610 €/km/MW.
- Stress-test threshold: 1430 €/km/MW.
- Always normalise total CAPEX by (P_electric + P_H2-equivalent).
- Tape cost share rises sharply with power rating — flag this in sensitivity.
