# Deep Research Update – Module 4 Cost – 2026-08-06

## Topic
Latest CAPEX unit costs, breakdowns and competitiveness thresholds for hybrid MgB₂/LH₂ and HTS hybrid energy pipelines.

## Key Findings
- TEMOA-Italy 2026: primary competitiveness threshold remains ≈ 610 €/km/MW; rises to ≈ 1430 €/km/MW if conventional transmission costs double.
- Feasibility often considered above ~540 MW total (electric + H₂-equivalent).
- MgB₂ wire: commonly 2–7 €/m or ≈ 5 $/kA·m.
- REBCO/HTS tape: still 80–200 € (or $)/kA·m at present; scale projections toward 30–50.
- Cryostat (LH₂-grade): ≈ 1.5 M€/km.
- Cable fabrication (structure + processing + labour): ≈ 0.5 M€/km in several models.
- Cooling stations: major driver (examples 50 M$/unit or 200 k€/kW of cooling power + ~400 k€ fixed per station).
- Supporting costs (labour, crossings, auxiliary): often 1.0–1.7 M€/km combined.
- Preliminary full cable CAPEX estimates cluster around 1.0–1.1 M€/km before power normalisation.
- Tape share of total CAPEX rises strongly with power rating (≈ 27 % at 1 GW → ≈ 58 % at multi-GW in HTS studies).
- Offshore configurations typically modelled ~1.7× onshore.

## Knowledge Upgrade for Expert Skill
- Primary go/no-go threshold: 610 €/km/MW.
- Stress-test threshold: 1430 €/km/MW.
- Default MgB₂ wire: 5 $/kA·m or 2–7 €/m (always show the range).
- Cryostat starting assumption: 1.5 M€/km for LH₂ systems.
- Always normalise CAPEX by (P_electric + P_H2-equivalent).
- Model cooling stations as a major, power-dependent cost centre.
- Run sensitivity on tape cost share (it grows with power).

## Sources
