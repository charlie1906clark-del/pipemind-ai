# Deep Research Update – Module 3 Safety – 2026-08-04

## Topic
Exact scientific basis of the 5 % leak size and current NFPA 2 / HyRAM+ methodology for bulk LH₂.

## Key Findings
- The 5 % of pipe flow-area leak size in NFPA 2 (2023) was selected after a full HyRAM+ quantitative risk assessment sensitivity study.
- Of 26 sensitivity cases, 21 produced equivalent fractional hole sizes below 5 % for all pipe diameters examined.
- Consequence distances are calculated from this 5 % leak using HyRAM+ models; the maximum hazard distance in each exposure group becomes the tabulated setback.
- Distances depend on pipe internal diameter and system pressure, not storage volume.
- Fire-barrier walls still permit a 50 % reduction for many Group 1 & 2 exposures.
- Complete O-ring failure on commercial bayonet connectors is also expected to stay ≤ 5 % of flow area.

## Knowledge Upgrade for Expert Skill
- Default representative leak size: 5 % of internal pipe flow area (NFPA 2 2023 / SAND justification).
- Always express setbacks as functions of pipe size + pressure.
- Include the fire-barrier 50 % reduction as a standard mitigation option.
- Model both immediate and delayed ignition probabilities from HyRAM+ defaults.
