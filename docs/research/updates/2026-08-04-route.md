# Deep Research Update – Module 1 Route – 2026-08-04

## Topic
Latest multi-criteria GIS and optimisation methods for hydrogen / energy pipeline corridor routing.

## Key Findings
- 2025 Sauerland (Germany) GIS+AHP study integrates climate-hazard layers (flood, erosion) and quantitatively scores infrastructure bundling with roads/existing networks. Conservation areas received the highest AHP weight (0.42).
- Steiner-tree + Obstacles Genetic Algorithm (StObGA) for multi-source multi-sink hydrogen networks delivers 20–40 % cost savings versus classic uniform-cost graph methods.
- Türkiye transcontinental H₂ corridor AHP weights: energy production 55 %, consumption 25 %, industry/population 14 %, renewables 6 %.
- Local-scale suitability rasters that penalise isolated placements while rewarding co-location with roads are becoming standard practice in European hydrogen corridor work.
- Least-cost path + AHP still outperforms conventional manually planned routes on length, crossings and average slope.

## Knowledge Upgrade for Expert Skill
- Always generate at least one “bundled” alternative that maximises co-location with motorways, A-roads and existing utility corridors.
- Include flood and erosion susceptibility as standard cost-surface layers.
- Conservation / protected-area layers should carry the highest weight in the AHP (or equivalent) by default.
- Prefer methods that can produce ranked alternatives rather than a single “optimal” path.
