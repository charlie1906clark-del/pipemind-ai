# Training Session 2026-08-11 – Modules 1 to 6
Company reports + scientific findings stored for Obsidian and direct use in future module building.

## Module 1 – Route
- Real TSO practice (Gasunie, OGE, Thyssengas, Northern Gas Networks) prioritises repurposing existing gas pipelines and co-location with existing corridors.
- Northern Gas Networks East Coast Hydrogen Pre-FEED uses multi-criteria analysis with existing infrastructure first.
- Scientific least-regret (UK Humber 2026) confirms Spine-First + anticipatory sizing cuts future regret dramatically.
- Conservation areas + climate-hazard layers remain highest-weight cost surfaces.

**Upgrade for building:** Prefer repurposing + co-location; always generate Spine-First alternative; score existing-infrastructure synergy explicitly.

## Module 2 – Cooling
- Latest 30 km MgB₂-LH₂ analyses still use ≤ 2 W/m design heat load.
- LOCA is the most severe transient (vacuum degradation can raise parasitic load 10–100×).
- Confirmed MLI at LH₂ temperature: 50-layer ≈ 9.92×10⁻⁵ W/(m·K).
- Actively cooled thermal shields continue to show 87–90 %+ heat-flux reduction.

**Upgrade for building:** Default heat leak 1.5–2.0 W/m; LOCA must model vacuum degradation; ACTS is high-value advanced option.

## Module 3 – Safety
- 5 % of internal pipe flow area remains the official NFPA 2 (2023) basis for bulk LH₂ and is still used by Sandia in 2025.
- 21 of 26 HyRAM+ cases fell below 5 %.
- Gaseous setbacks trending toward 3 % — keep methodologies strictly separate.
- Fire-barrier walls still give 50 % reduction for many exposures.

**Upgrade for building:** Default LH₂ leak size = 5 % of pipe flow area; distinguish gaseous vs liquid; offer barrier 50 % reduction.

## Module 4 – Cost
- TEMOA-Italy 2026 thresholds remain the strongest available: 610 €/km/MW primary, 1430 €/km/MW if conventional costs double.
- Detailed hybrid system cost tables show cooling stations and civil works as major non-tape drivers.
- MgB₂ wire commonly modelled ~5 $/kA·m.

**Upgrade for building:** Keep 610 / 1430 €/km/MW as primary decision thresholds; always normalise by (P_e + P_H2-eq); model cooling stations as power-dependent.

## Module 5 – Twin
- SHAPE (University of Calgary) remains the leading open XR+AI digital-twin research for hydrogen pipelines.
- Industrial digital twins (TÜV NORD LOOP, Siemens, TWI) are now being applied to hydrogen pipeline integrity and remaining-life assessment.
- Live bidirectional sensor streams and thermal/cooling alerts are mandatory design requirements.

**Upgrade for building:** Data model must support live bidirectional streams from day one; thermal/cooling anomalies first-class; architecture open for XR and integrity modules.

## Module 6 – Reports
- LLM-generated compliance matrices (requirement → status → justification → source) are now demonstrated in nuclear systems engineering.
- Assumption ledger / register is becoming a required binding artefact in high-assurance certificates.
- Traceability automation and continuous compliance monitoring are moving into practical tooling.

**Upgrade for building:** Every report must contain a machine-readable assumption register; automated compliance matrix is standard output; generate audit artefacts as a by-product of the calculation workflow.
