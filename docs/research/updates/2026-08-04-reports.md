# Deep Research Update – Module 6 Reports & Compliance – 2026-08-04

## Topic
Automated compliance, traceability and engineering documentation trends relevant to hydrogen and high-voltage projects.

## Key Findings
- LLM-based compliance matrix generation (requirement ↔ design decision + justification + source) is now demonstrated in nuclear systems engineering (MODELSWARD 2026).
- Knowledge-graph + automated compliance-checking engines are appearing in power-project management systems.
- “Assumption ledger” / assumption register is becoming a required binding artefact in high-assurance engineering certificates.
- Agile-V style frameworks show that full audit-evidence packs (requirements, traceability matrix, decision rationale, risk register, validation summary) can be generated as a by-product of the engineering workflow rather than as a separate documentation phase.
- Blockchain-based component certification and traceability is being explored for hydrogen refuelling station equipment.
