## Module 2 – Cooling & Cryogenics

**Upgrade:** Default heat leak 1.5–2.0 W/m; LOCA must include vacuum degradation; actively cooled shields high-value advanced option.

## Module 3 – Safety & Risk

**Upgrade:** Default LH₂ leak size = 5 % of internal pipe flow area; distinguish gaseous vs liquid methods; offer fire-barrier 50 % reduction.
