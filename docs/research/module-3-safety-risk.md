# Module 3 – Safety & Risk Simulator
## Complete Research Synthesis

### Purpose
Quantify risk for liquid-hydrogen releases, superconducting quenches, loss-of-flow and external impact events on hybrid pipelines, then produce clear recommendations for sensors and mitigation.

### Key Scenarios
- Small / medium / large hydrogen leaks (including the 5% flow-area representative size used in modern NFPA 2 work)
- Full-bore rupture
- Loss of cooling station or loss of flow
- Superconducting quench combined with hydrogen inventory
- External mechanical damage (road traffic, excavation)

### Methods
- Leak frequency and size characterisation (HyRAM+ style)
- Integral dispersion models for fast runs; CFD (FLACS etc.) for complex geometry later
- Consequence modelling (flammable cloud extent, heat flux, overpressure)
- Individual risk and simple societal risk metrics
- ALARP decision framework

### Standards and Guidance
- NFPA 2 (2023) with revised bulk LH₂ setback distances
- PRESLHY project recommendations and engineering tools
- ISO/TR 15916 (including emerging LH₂ chapter)
- EIGA documents, IEC 60079 series, functional safety standards (IEC 61508/61511)

### Outputs
- Risk heat-map overlaid on the route
- Recommended locations for hydrogen detectors, temperature/pressure transmitters and distributed fibre-optic sensing
- One-page management summary + detailed engineering report
- Complete assumption register for regulatory audit

### Key Sources
Sandia HyRAM+ and NFPA 2 justification reports, PRESLHY final deliverables, FLACS large-scale LH₂ studies, University of Bologna LOCA/loss-of-flow analyses for MgB₂-LH₂ cables, road-tunnel and process-plant CFD risk papers.
