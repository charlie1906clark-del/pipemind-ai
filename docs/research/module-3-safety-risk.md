# Module 3 Research – Safety & Risk Simulator

## Purpose
Quantify the risk of liquid-hydrogen releases, superconducting quenches, and loss-of-cooling events for hybrid pipelines.

## Key Scenarios
- Small, medium and large hydrogen leaks
- Full-bore rupture
- Loss of cooling station / loss of flow
- Superconducting quench
- External impact (road traffic, excavation)

## Methods
- Leak frequency and size characterisation (HyRAM+ style, often 5% of flow area as representative)
- Dispersion modelling (integral models first, CFD later for complex sites)
- Consequence modelling (flammable cloud, heat flux, overpressure)
- Individual and societal risk metrics
- ALARP principles

## Standards & Tools
- NFPA 2 (2023) LH₂ setback distances
- PRESLHY project recommendations
- ISO/TR 15916
- HyRAM+ toolkit
- PHAST / FLACS for higher fidelity

## Outputs the module should produce
- Risk heat-maps overlaid on the route
- Recommended sensor locations
- Clear residual risk summary
- Traceable assumptions for regulators
