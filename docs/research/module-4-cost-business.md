# Module 4 – Cost & Business Case Engine
## Complete Research Synthesis

### Purpose
Convert every technical output (length, crossings, stations, heat load, risk measures) into a transparent, auditable financial model that supports go/no-go decisions and comparison against conventional alternatives.

### Cost Structure from Literature
- Superconducting wire (MgB₂ typically lower cost per kA·m than REBCO)
- Copper former, electrical insulation, stainless-steel cryostat, multi-layer insulation
- Cable and pipeline fabrication
- Civil works, labour and major crossings
- Cooling stations (frequently the largest single capital item)
- Terminations and current leads
- Contingency factor (commonly 1.2)

### System-Level Thresholds
Italian TEMOA energy-system modelling found hybrid SCEPs become competitive below approximately 610 €/km/MW. If conventional transmission costs double, the threshold rises to around 1430 €/km/MW.

### Life-Cycle Metrics
CAPEX, annual OPEX (dominated by cryogenic electricity and maintenance), Net Present Value, Internal Rate of Return, levelised cost, simple payback. Comparisons against conventional underground cable + gaseous hydrogen pipeline, conventional cable + LH₂ tanker, and overhead line + trucking.

### Key Sources
Nature Communications Sustainability cost tables, TEMOA-Italy SCEP papers, SCARLET economic outputs, OSCaR HTS cable cost models, National Grid / IET underground cable reviews, European Hydrogen Backbone and HDSAM pipeline cost models.
