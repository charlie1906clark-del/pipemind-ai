# Module 4 Research – Cost & Business Case Engine

## Purpose
Turn technical outputs from the other modules into transparent CAPEX, OPEX, NPV and comparison against conventional alternatives.

## Key Cost Elements
- Superconducting wire (MgB₂ or REBCO)
- Cryostat and multi-layer insulation
- Cooling stations (often a major cost driver)
- Civil works and crossings
- Terminations and current leads
- Contingency (typically 20%)

## Important Thresholds from Literature
- Hybrid systems become competitive below ~610 €/km/MW in some Italian system studies
- Higher thresholds apply if conventional transmission costs rise

## Required Outputs
- Full CAPEX breakdown
- Annual OPEX (especially cooling electricity)
- 40-year NPV and IRR at user-chosen discount rate
- Side-by-side comparison vs conventional cable + hydrogen transport
- Tornado sensitivity charts
