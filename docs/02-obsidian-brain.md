# Obsidian Brain Structure for PipeMind AI

This is how you should organise your personal Obsidian vault so that all knowledge stays easy to find and never becomes messy.

## Recommended Vault Structure

```
PipeMind-Brain/
├── 00-Home.md
├── 01-Vision & Strategy/
│   ├── Vision.md
│   ├── 30-Day-Plan.md
│   ├── Long-Term-Roadmap.md
│   └── Key-Decisions.md
├── 02-Modules/
│   ├── Module-1-Route-Planner/
│   │   ├── Research.md
│   │   ├── Build-Plan.md
│   │   ├── Notes.md
│   │   └── Progress.md
│   ├── Module-2-Cooling/
│   ├── Module-3-Safety/
│   ├── Module-4-Cost/
│   ├── Module-5-Digital-Twin/
│   └── Module-6-Reports/
├── 03-Domain-Knowledge/
│   ├── Liquid-Hydrogen.md
│   ├── Superconducting-Cables.md
│   ├── SCARLET-Project.md
│   ├── Safety-Standards.md
│   └── Thermo-Hydraulics.md
├── 04-Customers & Outreach/
│   ├── Target-List.md
│   ├── Conversations-Log.md
│   └── Message-Templates.md
├── 05-Technical/
│   ├── Architecture-Decisions.md
│   ├── Tech-Stack.md
│   └── Open-Questions.md
└── 06-Daily/
    ├── Daily-Notes/
    └── Weekly-Reviews/
```

## How to use it

- Every time you learn something important, put it in the correct note
- Link notes heavily (Obsidian is built for linking)
- At the end of every day, write a short Daily Note
- Once a week, do a Weekly Review and update Progress.md files for each module
- Keep the `02-Modules` folder as the single source of truth for building

## Naming rules

- Use clear, consistent names
- Prefer short titles
- Always date important decisions
